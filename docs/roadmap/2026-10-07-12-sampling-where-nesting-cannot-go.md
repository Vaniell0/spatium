Stage 2.7, the estimator that was missing. `algebra/monte_carlo.hpp`: `monte_carlo(f, lo, hi, options)` over a box with random points, and `quasi_monte_carlo` with a Halton sequence of the first K primes (up to 32 dimensions), each copy shifted by an independent uniform vector mod 1 (Cranley-Patterson), the standard error being the spread of the copies' means -- which is what makes an error estimate possible for a deterministic low-discrepancy sequence at all. Both return the library's `IntegralResult`, and its `error_estimate` here is a standard error, one sigma, not a bound: the true error is within it about two times in three. The status is `Failed` where f is ever not finite (a NaN is not averaged), `Suspicious` where there are too few points (under 30) for a standard error to mean anything, and otherwise `Converged`, which for a sampler says only that an estimate with an error was formed. The generator is a seeded `std::mt19937_64` and the uniform numbers are formed from its 53 high bits, not from `std::uniform_real_distribution`, whose output differs between standard libraries, so a seed reproduces. It is for the dimensions where the door of `integrate`, which applies a one-dimensional rule per coordinate, stops being possible (n^K). Held against values it shares no path with: the integral of the product of 2 x_i over [0,1]^8 (1) within four standard errors, the sampler's 65536 points giving 0.012 and the Halton one 0.007 -- better, if not by much in eight dimensions on 4096 points per shift, and in two dimensions (sin(xy) over the unit square against nested quadrature) more than five times better; the volume of a 5-ball by an indicator, 8 pi^2/15; the area of a torus from its metric's volume element, 4 pi^2 R r; reproducibility by seed; and a `Dual` carried through (the derivative of the integral of p x with respect to p, 1/2, within its error). An expectation that was a guess, not a measurement -- the Halton sequence three times better than random at 65536 points in eight dimensions -- did not hold, and the test asserts what was measured. Not done: a sampler by the measure of a space (`sample_surface_uniform` is by area and returns points; the estimator over a surface that weighs by them), adaptive importance sampling, and a Sobol sequence.
