The page had drifted from the reference the owner picked, `answer-sheet.html`. Cards spanned the full width. The verdict came before the result. Sections had no rules between them, and steps had their label beside the formula. The keyboard made the page longer than its content.

The calculator is now one cell of the reference, copied value for value:
- a column 820 wide;
- the ask row: the prompt, the line with no box of its own, and a 2 px accent rule while focused;
- the result, with the verdict under it;
- sections ruled off, titles in capitals and the tag on the right;
- the chain numbered by a counter, what a step does above its formula, dashed apart;
- facts in mono, root tables and how it was found as the reference sets them, and its rules at 480 px.

The plot is placed before how it was found, and history entries are the same cells.

The keyboard is a window of its own over the page. It takes no room from it, moves by its title bar, and comes back where it was left.

Checked by screenshots of the reference and the page side by side at 900 px.
