title: The infinity probe, and what scouting its classes found
---
Not enumeration -- there are 2^64 doubles -- but the classes whose behaviour differs: a zero step, NaN in the point, the tangent and the step, an infinite step, and two steps too long to answer; the invariants are that a zero step stays put, NaN and infinity never come out as a finite point, and a long step returns. The matrix is 864 cells, 811 at L2 or above, and the probe's row is L3 on every space and scalar. Getting there it found, or the scout of its classes found, seven defects, each now held by a test. `ParametricSurface` turned NaN and an infinite step into a finite point of the surface (every comparison with NaN is false, so the nearest-parameter search kept its first grid point). `parametric.hpp` and `implicit.hpp` called `std::fmod`, `std::abs`, `std::cos` and the like qualified, which blocks ADL: the reason the torus chart and the sphere level set were `L0` over `Real50`, `Dual` and `Dual2` since the matrix began. `Sphere::exp_map` and `Hyperbolic::exp_map` returned `p` for a zero tangent on a `speed == 0` that compares a Dual's value alone, so d/dv at v = 0 was 0 where it is t -- now `cos_sinc_of_square`, even functions of the squared angle with a series near zero whose threshold follows the scalar. `std::numeric_limits<Dual<T>>` was the primary template, so `quiet_NaN()`, `infinity()`, `max()` and `epsilon()` of a Dual were all zero: `MetricChart`'s "no answer" was the origin over a Dual, and the search above started from `max() == 0` and never improved -- now specialised, at any depth. `Hyperbolic::distance` clamped with `max(x, 1)`, which is NaN for a double NaN and 1 for a Boost one, so a NaN point sat at distance 0 from the origin over `Real50`. `integrate_extrapolated` is bounded in evaluations (200 000) so a step of 1e10 is refused at once. And three `Real50` compile failures went with them: `solve_quadratic` with expression arguments in SPD, an `initializer_list` of Boost expressions in `find_params`, and a third-party cylinder in the probes. What is left red: the nine rows of first-order `exp`/`log` on erased surfaces (stage 2.3), and SPD affine-invariant over `Dual` in the derivative cell. The classes it did not reach are the lint of plan stage 1b item 13.
