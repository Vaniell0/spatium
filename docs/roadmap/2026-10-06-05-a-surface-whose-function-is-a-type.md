Stage 2.3. `ParametricSurface<T, F>` takes the function as a second template parameter, by default the erased `std::function` it always was, so `ParametricSurface<T>` means what it meant and forty files are untouched. `make_parametric<T>(f, domain, ...)` with a generic lambda keeps it a type: it can be called on a `Dual`, so the partials -- hence `normal_at`, `area_element`, `find_params` -- are exact where the erased form takes difference quotients (1e-7), and `exp_map` and `log_map` are the geodesics of the induced metric g = J^T J, handed as a callable on any scalar to `MetricChart` over (u, v), with the tangent put into the chart's basis by the normal equations and, on a periodic chart, the nearest representative of the other point. A typed surface has no member `distance` (that was the chord), so `core/access.hpp` derives |log|; the chord stays as `chord_distance`. The erased form stays a first-order retraction -- exp(log) is not the identity on it, and the matrix keeps that row red beside the typed one -- and `.erased()` is the one explicit boundary, taken by `chart_of` for the DSL, by tessellate and by the ray code. Held against what the code cannot know: on the torus a meridian and the outer equator are geodesics at known speeds (to 1e-12), the geodesic distance along a meridian is q dv, Clairaut's quantity is conserved along an oblique geodesic, `exp(log)` is the identity to 1e-9, the distance exceeds the chord, a seam in a periodic chart is crossed the short way, and d/dt of the flow at t = 0 is the velocity through a `Dual` scalar -- AD through the induced metric, its Christoffel symbols, the extrapolated flow and f. A sphere in latitude-longitude gives NaN at its pole, where the metric does not invert, not a wrong point. In the matrix the typed torus is green over double, float, long double and Dual; it rides on four scalars (a flow of nested Duals over fifty digits is minutes a cell), double held against long double.
