`x^2 - 4 > 0` was refused: a row was an equation or an expression. A root's relation may now be `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=` beside `=` -- in a document (`x: x^2 - 4 >= 0 on [-5, 5]`), in one line, and in the rows of the editor (Less, LessEqual, Greater, GreaterEqual) -- and its answer is the set where it holds, by the interval method (`interval_method`, a route of `evaluate`). The axis is cut where the inequality is undecided on a piece, computed in intervals (`prove_on`), until each piece is proved to hold it or not; the pieces nothing was proved of are where it changes, each a root of its left side less its right, proved alone there by Newton's interval step (`roots_on`) and an end of the set, or a break where it has no value. An end that is a short fraction is checked to be one in fractions -- the value is zero there, or has none -- so the ends read 2 and -2, not 2.000000000000000 and -1.9999999999999998. The answer is the set in LaTeX, (-inf, -2) U (2, inf), the empty set or every real number, a root in a bracket where equality holds, a break never; the sheet carries the axis, and the page draws it as the method is drawn on paper: the points evenly apart, the sign of the left side less the right over each piece, the set shaded, a root filled where it belongs to the set.
