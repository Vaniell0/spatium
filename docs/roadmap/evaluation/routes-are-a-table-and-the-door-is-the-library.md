**Routes as a table.** A route was handled in four places: chosen by a chain of `if` in `evaluate`, and written out by three `switch`es in `respond` (the JSON, the sheet, the chains), each route's result in its own optional field, and the sheet read the answer's own JSON back by field name. Now a route is a row of `route_table` (`answer/evaluate.hpp`): its name, its caption, what it applies to and how it runs, the rows in the order of preference that `take` reads, the one place the choice is made. Each candidate carries the steps its route ran, written as it ran them -- the cuts of every root proved and Newton's step landing in each, the ends of an inequality's set, the bisection -- where the sheet used to compose them afterwards; a check is a chain of its own over the same solution (Vieta's formulas over a closed form's coefficients and roots), not a step told apart by its label. The sheet and the graph are built from the typed evaluation. Held by 105 answers -- the gallery's, the reference's, the tests', and the engines rational, strict, decimal and real50 -- byte for byte the same before and after.

**What is not a number.** The checks found answers that said what is not so. `max(1, inf)` was exact and 1: a fraction spoiled by an infinity is below every fraction, so the larger of 1 and it was 1; `min`, `max` and `less` of fractions now spoil. `1/(1/0)` was 0, `less(1/0, 1)` exact 0: the empty interval became the whole line when divided by, zero when multiplied by zero, a number in `min` and `max`; each now stays empty. `2^2000` was "within 10⁰ of zero"; it is past the largest double now, and its step `2^{2000} > 10^{308}`. `1/0`, `0/0`, `sqrt(-1)` were computed in double; each is refused naming the operation that has no value ("the quotient 1 / 0 has none"), and the chain of steps ends there, where it printed `0 \pm 10^{-2147483648}`. An infinity or a NaN in an answer was written `inf` into the JSON, which a page cannot parse; it is `null`. A synthesis in more than two pieces listed none of them.

**An infinity is a limit.** A value that reads `inf` is answered by a route of its own, first in the table: every inf is one x growing without bound, and the value is its limit, computed as a Laurent series in 1/x (`algebra/series.hpp`) -- arithmetic on the chart at infinity, where no infinity enters an operation. `max(1, inf)` is infinite and grows like x, `inf * 0` and `inf - inf` are 0 (the limit of x - x), `inf / inf` is 1, `sqrt(inf)` grows like x^{1/2}, `(inf^2 + 1)/(3 inf^2 - inf)` is 1/3, `sin(1/inf) * inf` is 1. Its steps are the leading term and the limit. A power with a series in its exponent is `exp(g log f)`; a series, like an interval, runs only the elementary functions of the interpreter.

**The door into the library.** `respond`, `evaluate`, `closed_form`, `query` and `synthesis` answer a document and run no search, so they leave `rsc/spatium` for `include/spatium/answer/`, namespace `spatium::answer`; the library still includes nothing of `rsc/`. `rsc/core` (the graph, the tree search) stays generic, and `rsc/spatium` keeps the finder. Two different `rsc::Route`s, the graph's edges and the evaluation's routes, no longer share a namespace. Held by the full suite (1475 of 1475), the WebAssembly core against the host, and the reference and gallery written the same.
