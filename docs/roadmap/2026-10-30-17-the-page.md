`site/` is now a page: `index.html`, `style.css` and `app.js`, static, with KaTeX 0.16.22 self-hosted in `site/vendor/katex/` (MIT). Four tabs -- Solve, Gallery, Reference, Scene -- with addresses `#solve?q=...`, `#gallery/<id>`, `#reference`, `#scene`; on a narrow screen the tabs move to the bottom. The page draws at once and loads the core after; a small note in the corner says when it has loaded. The settings hold the theme (system, light, dark), the text size, the digits shown, rendered formulas or their LaTeX source, the on-screen keyboard, whether the core is kept in the browser's cache, and a check for a newer core (by the SHA-256 of the wasm).

Solve reads the question line through the core as it is typed: the formula, or the parse error with its column underlined in the text as typed. The word under the caret is explained from the reference the library wrote. An answer shows the result with Copy link, the steps of a closed-form solution as the solver recorded them, the check by Vieta's formulas, the certificate of a synthesised answer (strength, norm, grid, scalar, where it was computed), each piece and a Chebyshev polynomial's coefficients, or the refusal with its reason. Focus leaves the question and the result alone. Nothing on the page is computed or written by the page: what it shows is the JSON of `rsc::respond`, `gallery.json` and `reference.json`.

What the page showed sent four changes into the library. LaTeX writes a number or a fraction next to a name (`2x^{2}`, `\frac{1}{6} p`) and a typed minus as a minus (`-p \cdot x^{2}`, `1 - u`; the reader builds -a as -1 * a, which the text keeps for the round trip). A step's label is plain text. The preview's error column counts the leading `solve` or `integrate`. A refusal names the parameter in the shortest digits (`no value at p = 0.5`). And the gallery's cards are asked at the tolerance a live answer has, so a card and the same question typed on the page give the same answer: the Gaussian card is now a Chebyshev polynomial of degree 16 below 1e-14, where it was degree 8 below 1e-7 at a looser tolerance.
