`gpu/` held the first GPU renderer: CUDA kernels for a Schwarzschild and one Kerr metric in Boyer-Lindquist coordinates, Christoffel symbols derived by a sympy script and transcribed by hand, and three programs that checked them. It is removed with the option that built it (`SPATIUM_CUDA`), its preset, its Nix shell and its README row. The Vulkan path does what it did and more: `blackhole_live` traces one hole or a pair from the metric's IR with exact derivatives, on any device with a Vulkan driver, and its shadow check measures 0.16798 rad against 0.16812 expected at D = 30 M (a third of a pixel).

What a device reads moves to `include/spatium/render/gpu/`: the std430 layouts, the packed scene, the linear BVH and its kernels, instances moved on the device, and the trace, splat and black-hole shaders. `render/` keeps the CPU ray tracer and the cooked scene both read. Every file moved has a consumer (`donut_live`, `blackhole_live`, `src/viewer/gpu_lbvh.cpp`, `tests/test_lbvh.cpp`); nothing in it was unused.

`docs/gpu.md` describes the path as it stands: which layer is where, how each shader is held against its host twin, fp32 on a device with no fp64, and the two programs. `docs/gpu-abi-design.md` records the CUDA derivation gap as closed by the removal.
