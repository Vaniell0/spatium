title: A quadrature that says how far to trust itself
---
A dispute about the library's integration support became an audit, run rather than asserted. Measured against closed forms with mpmath at 60 digits (a local oracle, not in the build; `wolframscript` is not installed and the Wolfram API needs a key): over `Real50` at the default tolerance `integrate` agrees with the closed form to 39 digits on sin over [0, pi], 42 on exp over [0, 1], and 11 on sqrt over [0, 1], whose derivative is singular at 0; `exp(-x)` over [0, inf), `1/sqrt(x)` and `log x` over [0, 1], and exp(-x^2) over the line returned NaN -- not a defect of the rule but of the model, an interval as a finite pair sampled at its ends, whose cure is a change of variable (tanh-sinh, exp-sinh, sinh-sinh) and so a domain that is a type. What changed now is the shape of the answer: `integrate_with_error` returns `IntegralResult<T>` -- the value, the rule's error estimate (sum of the accepted panels' |two halves - whole| / 15), the evaluations spent, and a `status` -- `Converged`, `Suspicious` (tolerance met but the estimate did not fall between the first two levels as a smooth integrand's does: an oscillation the first levels cannot resolve), `DepthCapped`, `Failed`; one bool had joined the good case and the dangerous one. The interval is never accepted on its first look. It is named an estimate and not a bound on purpose: nothing proves it, and a bound is `Certified<T>`'s, from interval arithmetic. `integrate` is the value alone, unchanged. A bound or a value that is NaN or infinite now stops the rule at once -- a NaN used to walk the recursion to its depth limit of 20, 2^20 evaluations, minutes over `Real50` -- and the limits of one rule are stated in tests: a NaN lying between the nodes of a constant integrand is never seen, and x^0.7 at a loose tolerance is accepted as Converged with a true error 5.7 times its estimate -- no status of one rule sees that; a second witness that samples differently does (Gauss-Kronrod, tanh-sinh), and the disagreement between witnesses is the signal. Not done and in the plan: Gauss-Kronrod with its own estimate (the pair with Simpson: their difference no larger than the sum of the estimates), the domains `Finite`, `HalfLine`, `WholeLine`, tanh-sinh, `volume_element` beside `christoffel`, `integrate_surface`, Gauss-Bonnet, a Monte Carlo estimator.
