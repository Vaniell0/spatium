Stage 2.5, the first of the representations. `spaces/pullback.hpp`: `pullback_metric<K, M>(f, ambient)` is the metric g_ij = <d_i f, d_j f> of the space given by coordinates x in R^K and a map f into an ambient space, the partials exact by a `Dual` seeded along each coordinate; the ambient metric is a callable `(point, u, v) -> scalar` generic over the scalar (`EuclideanMetric`, `MinkowskiMetric`, or any), and the result is what `spaces::metric_chart` takes, so the pulled-back space has Christoffel symbols, geodesics, exp and log with nothing more written. `ParametricSurface`'s induced metric J^T J was a private copy of this for K = 2, M = 3 and a Euclidean ambient; it is now the Euclidean case of it, 14 lines shorter, and the 51 surface, typed-surface and metric-chart tests are unchanged. Held by four checks that share nothing with the code: the torus's first fundamental form in closed form (g_uu = (R + r cos v)^2, g_vv = r^2, 1e-13); the unit sphere through the stereographic map against the kappa = 1 family's conformal factor, and the hyperbolic plane through the Poincare map against the kappa = -1 family's -- the second from a Lorentzian ambient metric on the hyperboloid, the same code, 1e-12; and the distance of the pulled-back sphere derived by geodesic shooting (Christoffel symbols by Dual, an extrapolated flow) against the family's closed form, 1e-7. Not done: `restrict`, `quotient` and `compactify`, and a `Pullback<S, F>` space type (points, `contains`, exp, log) over this metric rather than the metric alone.
