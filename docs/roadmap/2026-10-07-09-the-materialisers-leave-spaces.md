Stage 2.4. `spaces/parametric.hpp` and `spaces/implicit.hpp` included `mesh/mesh.hpp` for one reason each -- the free functions that turn a surface into triangles, `tessellate` for a parametric surface and `marching_cubes` for an implicit one -- while `mesh/primitives.hpp` and `mesh/conform.hpp` included `spaces/` for the surfaces their generators stand on, so the two directories depended on each other and `docs/architecture.md` listed it as a fact of life. Both functions (and the 430 lines of marching-cubes tables) now live in `mesh/tessellate.hpp`: a surface described by a function becomes triangles only there, at the edge, when something asks (drawing, a baseline to compare a method against), which is the rule the physics already states. The names stay in namespace `spatium`, so `tessellate(surface, nu, nv)` and `marching_cubes(surface, n)` are found by ADL and no call changed; what changed is an include in the seven files that call them and in the `mesh.hpp` umbrella. Measured by the graph that CI regenerates from the real `#include`s: the domain-level edge `spaces -> mesh` is gone (60 edges, one fewer) and no header of `spaces/` includes one of `mesh/`; the architecture note that called the mutual dependency genuine is rewritten. Like `primitives.hpp`, the new header stays outside the `spatium.mesh` module, which a consumer of modules includes directly; with the cycle gone the module graph can be turned the right way round, which is not done here. Not done: `primitives.hpp`, `conform.hpp` and `operations.hpp` into the mesh module (they were kept out by this cycle and by geometry's), and the stage 2.5 representations.
